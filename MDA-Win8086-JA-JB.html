      MOV XCHG LEA PUSH POP PUSHF POPF LAHF SAHF XLAT IN OUT CBW CWD

      ADD ADC SUB SBB CMP INC DEC NEG MUL IMUL DIV IDIV

      AND OR XOR NOT TEST SHL SAL SHR SAR ROL ROR RCL RCR

      CLC STC CMC CLD STD CLI STI NOP HLT INT

      Memory operands: [BX+SI+2], BYTE PTR CS:[SI], LABEL[SI]. Data: DB DW DUP ? 'text', EQU, OFFSET.

      Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter assembles and runs, F10 steps, Tab indents.
